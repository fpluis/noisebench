The five measures behind the score, one section each: what it is, the formula it comes from, and every model's value. All five are within-model — each compares a model against its own other answers, so nothing here depends on what the other nineteen said or on how a market resolved. Lower is better throughout, and zero is attainable in all five.

Negation error

The Overview carries the absolute version, which is what the score uses. Signs cancel across models and across questions; absolute errors do not, which is why the score is built on the magnitude and this chart is only here to say where the magnitude comes from.

Negation disagreement

The two couples ask different things. Which is likelier compares the pair directly, so negating both sides must flip the answer. Do they sum past 100% compares one outcome against the other's complement, and negating both sides of that must flip too. Both are forced by logic alone, whatever the model believes — see Details for why the two together are sufficient to audit all four answers.
