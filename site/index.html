Overview 1 · Noise 2 · Yes/No bias

How much do language models disagree with themselves when forecasting? Twenty models were asked 100 Polymarket questions, each in its own phrasing and in its negation, four times each, plus 50 ranked pairs — 24,000 forecasts in total. Every measure here is about noise and internal consistency. Nothing is scored against how the markets actually resolved; where a market price appears it is an input describing what the crowd thought when the question was asked.

Findings so far

Goal 1 · Noise
—
Occasion noise — the same model, the same prompt, a different answer — is the largest of the three components, and system noise exceeds the real spread between markets.

Coverage

Parse failures are not spread evenly, so the noise analysis reports a strictly balanced subset alongside its primary fit. A model that answered fewer questions is measured on fewer of them.
